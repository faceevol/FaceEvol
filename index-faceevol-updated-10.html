FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What works on FaceEvol right now?

The current beta focuses on video face swap: upload a face photo and MP4 video, select a clip up to 20 seconds, process it with AI, preview the result and download the finished video.

Can I explore different ages?

Yes. The Age Preview section includes visual presets for ages 5, 15, 30, 50 and 75, plus the existing custom age slider from 0 to 100.

What video can I upload?

For the beta, use an MP4 source file smaller than 300 MB. You can then select any section up to 20 seconds for processing.

Will 2K and 4K be available?

Higher-resolution output is planned. The current beta prioritizes reliability first, with 2K, 4K and enhancement features coming later.

Is FaceEvol free?

FaceEvol currently offers beta access while the core experience is being developed. Future paid plans may add higher limits, quality and processing options.
