FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What can I create with FaceEvol?

FaceEvol includes 10 focused experiences: AI Portrait Creator, Photo to Video, Age Transform, Photo Face Swap, Video Face Swap, Multiple Photo Face Swap, Multiple Video Face Swap, Photo Enhance, Video Enhance and the signature AI Face Evolution life-journey video.

What photos work best for face swap?

Use a clear, front-facing source face with good lighting and the full face visible. Avoid blur, sunglasses, masks, heavy shadows and extreme side angles. A target photo with a clearly visible face and a similar angle usually gives a more natural result.

How is AI Face Evolution different from Age Transform?

Age Transform creates one still image at a chosen age. AI Face Evolution creates a short cinematic life journey that moves from childhood through adulthood to older age while keeping the same identity recognizable.

How do multiple face swaps work?

Multiple Photo Face Swap labels target people left to right so you can choose exactly who each source replaces. Multiple Video Face Swap uses the same left-to-right mapping, preserves the uploaded source clip, and swaps only the selected faces.

Which ages can I choose?

The five visual presets are ages 5, 15, 30, 50 and 75. You can also use the custom slider to choose any target age from 0 to 100.

Can I download my Age Transform result?

Yes. When an Age Transform finishes, tap “Download Image” below the result to save it to your device.

Can I download my photo face swap?

Yes. When the photo face swap is complete, tap “Download Image” below the result to save it directly to your device.

How does Photo to Video work?

Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP photo, type a short description of the motion you want, then tap “Generate Short Video.” FaceEvol creates a short clip that you can preview and download.

How does Photo Enhance work?

Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP photo, choose 2x or 4x enhancement, then tap “Enhance Photo.” FaceEvol creates a clearer higher-resolution result that you can compare with the original and download.

What video can I upload?

Use an MP4 source file smaller than 300 MB. Video Face Swap supports a selected section up to 20 seconds. Video Enhance supports a selected section up to 5 seconds.

How does Video Enhance work?

Upload an MP4 up to 60 seconds, use the trim handles to select any section up to 5 seconds, then choose 1080p or 4K and 30 or 60 FPS. FaceEvol enhances only the selected clip.

Do the demos call the AI models?

No. Demo mode loads only pre-generated sample images and videos stored for the website. Opening, replaying or comparing a demo does not contact the generation APIs and does not consume FaceEvol credits.

Is FaceEvol free?

The FaceEvol demo gallery is free to explore and uses pre-generated stored media, so viewing demos does not use credits or start AI processing. Creating with your own photo or video requires purchased credits. FaceEvol launches with zero free AI-generation credits.

Will I need an account?

No account is required to browse the site or explore the pre-generated demos. An account is required when you want to buy credits and create with your own photo or video.

How do FaceEvol credits work?

Your account stores a secure server-side credit balance. Demo viewing costs 0 credits. Real AI creations deduct the tool cost when a generation starts, and the cost is shown in the interface. Verified failed or canceled jobs are restored automatically.

How does FaceEvol handle my uploads?

Your uploads are processed to create the result you request. FaceEvol is designed to minimize unnecessary storage, while third-party hosting and AI providers may process content as needed to provide the service. See the Privacy Policy below for more detail.

Where can I find the legal information?

Privacy, Terms, Refunds and Contact information are available in the Legal & Support section at the bottom of this page.
