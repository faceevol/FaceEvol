FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What can I create with FaceEvol?

FaceEvol combines Age Transformation and Video Face Swap in one workflow. You can use your original face for a swap, or transform the face first and continue directly into the video tool.

Which ages can I choose?

The five visual presets are ages 5, 15, 30, 50 and 75. You can also use the custom slider to choose any target age from 0 to 100.

Can I use an age-transformed face directly in Video Face Swap?

Yes. When an age transformation finishes, use the “Use this face in Video Face Swap” button to continue without manually downloading and uploading the result again.

What video can I upload?

For the beta, use an MP4 source file smaller than 300 MB. You can then select any section up to 20 seconds for processing.

Will HD and 4K video enhancement be available?

Yes, that is on the FaceEvol roadmap. The current beta prioritizes the connected creation workflow first, with HD and 4K enhancement planned as upcoming quality options.

Is FaceEvol free?

FaceEvol currently offers beta access while the core experience is being refined. Future paid plans may add higher limits, quality and processing options.
